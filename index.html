WHAT FIKE FIXES

Six trades, one visit.

Most jobs fall into one of these. If yours doesn't, ask anyway — chances are it's still a fix, not a specialty.

TV mounting

Studs found, level checked, cables tucked away. Any wall, any size TV.

Trimwork

Baseboards, casing, and crown molding, cut and caulked clean.

Furniture assembly

Flat-pack furniture, put together right the first time, hardware included.

Painting

Interior touch-ups to full rooms, prepped and taped properly.

Door & lock repair

Sticking doors, worn hinges, and locks that need adjusting or swapping.

Computer repairs

Slow machines, failed boots, and setup help — the fix most handymen wave off.
